Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about my homelab, technologies, and learning approach.

Why do you maintain a homelab?

My homelab provides a safe environment to build, deploy, troubleshoot, automate and document enterprise Windows infrastructure without impacting production systems.

Which technologies do you focus on?

My primary focus is Windows Server, Active Directory, Hyper-V, Microsoft 365, PowerShell, networking, storage, backup and infrastructure automation.

How often is the lab updated?

The environment evolves continuously as I learn new technologies, complete certifications, test enterprise features, and build new infrastructure projects.

Are the projects based on real-world practices?

Yes. Every project follows enterprise deployment principles including planning, implementation, validation, documentation and continuous improvement.
